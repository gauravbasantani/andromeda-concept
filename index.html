Ideas

Show pressure on the camera rim

Your camera view already uses a clock face. If the robot can sense contact, the rim shows where and how hard the tool touches, in color and in words.

Draw the sphincter limit on the map

EUS Block protects the external sphincter, but it lives in settings. On the map, the surgeon can see the limit coming.

Say what Next task will do

One plain sentence before the robot moves, and a one-second hold so it never starts by accident. Abort stays where it is.

Decisions made for surgeons in the room

  • Eyes stay on the cameraPressure lives on the rim of the view the surgeon is already watching, not in a panel off to the side.
  • Never color aloneEvery color also has a word (light, firm, too firm) and a thicker line, for glare, distance and color blindness.
  • Readable from a step backFew words, large type, and the same clock language surgeons already use to orient.
  • Big targets for gloved handsThe buttons that matter are large, and nothing important sits in a corner that is easy to miss.
  • No surprisesThe robot says what it will do before it moves, and a one-second hold means a brushed screen cannot start it.
  • Stopping is instantAbort stays one tap, always in the same place. Starting takes intent, stopping never does.

What I would ask surgeons first

  • Should pressure show all the time, or only when contact gets firm?
  • Is the external sphincter the main limit you watch for, or are there others?
  • Is a one-second hold quick enough in the middle of a case?
